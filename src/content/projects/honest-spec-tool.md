Honest Spec came out of frustration with comparison sites that bury the useful numbers under SEO filler and affiliate buttons. This tool does one thing: line up two products and tell you, plainly, where they actually differ in ways you'll feel.

## How to try it

1. Paste or search two products.
2. Pick what you care about most — battery, repairability, performance.
3. Read the side-by-side, with marketing terms stripped out.

It won't tell you which one you'll *love*. It'll tell you which one is objectively better at the things you said mattered — and leave the rest to you.
