Hermes started as a way to answer one question honestly: *what does it actually take for an AI agent to research something well?* Most agent demos hide the messy middle. Hermes does the opposite — it shows the plan, the searches, the dead ends, and the bill.

## How to try it

1. Open the app and type a research question.
2. Review the plan it proposes, then let it run.
3. Watch the steps and the cost meter as it works.
4. Read the report — and click through to the sources to check it.

It's deliberately unpolished in places. That's the point: this is what a working agent really looks like under the hood, costs and limits included.
